Equations are falling from the sky. Type the answer, pop the drop — before it hits the water.

Qatra Challenge turns mental math into a fast, satisfying reaction game. Simple to start, hard to master, and every round takes about a minute.

💧 HOW IT WORKS
Each drop carries a sum. Type the answer and it bursts. Miss one and it splashes into the sea. The storm gets faster as you improve.

⚡ GAME MODES
• Journey — 100 levels across 10 worlds, from simple sums to boss storms
• Time Challenge — how many drops can you pop in 60 seconds?
• Survival — endless rain, three hearts, how long can you last?
• Daily Challenge — the same drops for every player on Earth, once a day. Share your score grid with friends!
• Free Practice — choose operations and difficulty, no pressure
• Friend Battle — two players, one phone

🤝 PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Challenge a friend with a code on WhatsApp: they play exactly the same drops and reply with their result
• Same-phone room: 2–6 players take turns, racing the ghosts of those before
• Face to face: two players at once on a split screen
• Global and friends leaderboards with Google Play Games

🏆 PROGRESS
Stars, badges, daily missions, Google Play Games achievements and leaderboards, and colourful looks that also colour your drops in game.

🧠 SMART & FAIR
Questions adapt to your level. The whole game works offline with no account. Purchases are cosmetic only: no pay-to-win.

📚 LEARN
A calm learning section with step-by-step lessons for addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, percentages and more.

Works offline for solo play. Arabic and English, plus Spanish, Portuguese, French, Turkish, Indonesian and Hindi.
